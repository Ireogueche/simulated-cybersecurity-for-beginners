# 🛡️ Online Safety

## What Is Online Safety?

Online safety means protecting yourself, your personal information, and your devices when using the internet.

## Why Is Online Safety Important?

It helps protect you from online scams, identity theft, cyberbullying, and other online threats.

## Tips for Staying Safe Online

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Never share your passwords with anyone.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links.
- Be careful when using public Wi-Fi.
- Do not share sensitive personal information with strangers.
- Enable two-factor authentication on your accounts.
- Keep your devices and applications updated.
- Check that websites are trustworthy before entering personal information.
