# 🎭 Social Engineering

## What Is Social Engineering?

Social engineering is when someone tricks or manipulates people into sharing private information or doing something unsafe.

Instead of attacking a computer, scammers often target the people using it.

## Common Tricks

- **Fear:** Threatening to block your account.
- **Urgency:** Pressuring you to act quickly.
- **Impersonation:** Pretending to be a bank worker or trusted person.
- **Fake Offers:** Promising prizes or rewards to get your details.

## How to Stay Safe

- Never share your password or PIN.
- Do not rush into making decisions.
- Verify people's identity before trusting them.
- Contact organisations through official channels.
- Be careful with unexpected calls and messages.
