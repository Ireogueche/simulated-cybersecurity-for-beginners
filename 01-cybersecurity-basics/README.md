# 🛡️ Cybersecurity Basics

## What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, phones, networks and personal information from online attacks.

It helps keep our devices, accounts and data safe.

## Why Is Cybersecurity Important?

We use the internet every day to communicate, study, work and share information.

Cybersecurity helps protect us from:

- Hackers trying to access private information.
- People stealing passwords and accounts.
- Online scams and fraud.
- Viruses and harmful software.
- Unauthorised access to devices and networks.

## Basic Cybersecurity Practices

- Use strong passwords.
- Avoid suspicious links.
- Keep your software updated.
- Lock your devices when not in use.
- Do not share private information with strangers.
- Use trusted websites and applications.

## Common Cybersecurity Terms

- **Threat:** Something that could cause harm to a computer or its data.
- **Vulnerability:** A weakness that could be used in an attack.
- **Attack:** An attempt to access, damage or steal information.
- **Protection:** Steps taken to keep systems and information safe.
