SCENARIO 01 · BEGINNER
REAL-WORLD SITUATION

An unattended computer

You are walking through a shared office or learning space when you notice an unattended computer. The computer is switched on, unlocked and displaying someone's personal workspace.

No one is currently sitting at the computer. You realise that anyone nearby could potentially access information or make changes.

What is the most responsible action to take?

Key takeaway

An unattended, unlocked computer can expose sensitive information. Always respect other people's privacy. Where appropriate and authorised, secure the computer and report the situation to its owner or responsible staff.
