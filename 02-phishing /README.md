# 🎣 Phishing Awareness

## What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of online scam where criminals use fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into sharing private information.

They may try to steal passwords, bank details or personal information.

## Common Signs of Phishing

- Messages that create fear or panic.
- Links from unknown sources.
- Requests for passwords or bank details.
- Spelling mistakes or strange email addresses.
- Offers that seem too good to be true.

## How to Stay Safe

- Avoid clicking suspicious links.
- Never share your password.
- Check the sender's email address.
- Visit official websites directly.
- Report suspicious messages.
